How to Keep Things Neat & Clean

Speaker: Environmental Consultant

🔍 Regular Monitoring

Check your dashboard daily, review weekly summaries, and set conservation goals consistently

♻️ Waste Management

Segregate waste at source, recycle properly, compost organic waste, and dispose hazardous materials safely

⚡ Energy Conservation

Turn off lights in unused areas, use efficient appliances, optimize HVAC, and maximize natural lighting

💧 Water Conservation

Fix leaks immediately, install water-efficient fixtures, collect rainwater, and monitor patterns

Best Practices for Cleanliness

🧹 Clean Workspace Maintenance

  • Keep work areas organized and clutter-free
  • Use eco-friendly, biodegradable cleaning products
  • Reduce paper usage through digital documentation
  • Implement proper storage systems

👥 Team Engagement

  • Create awareness programs about sustainability
  • Set team and departmental sustainability goals
  • Recognize and celebrate green initiatives
  • Share best practices across teams

📅 Regular Audits

  • Conduct monthly environmental audits
  • Review consumption reports quarterly
  • Update targets based on progress
