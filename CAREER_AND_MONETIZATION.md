## 📈 Part 4: Customer Acquisition Channels

### Organic (Low Cost)
1. **GitHub** - 30% of early customers
2. **Hacker News** - 15% of early customers
3. **Product Hunt** - 10% of early customers
4. **Blog/Content** - 20% ongoing
5. **Word of Mouth** - 15% ongoing
6. **Reddit** - 10% early adopters

### Paid (Medium Cost)
1. **Google Ads** - $3-5 per click, 5-10% conversion
2. **LinkedIn Ads** - $1-3 per click, 2-5% conversion
3. **Facebook/Instagram** - $0.5-2 per click, 1-3% conversion
4. **Industry publications** - $500-5,000 per month

### Partnerships (Variable Cost)
1. **System integrators** - 20-30% commission
2. **Resellers** - 15-25% margin
3. **Technology partners** - Revenue sharing
4. **Environmental consultants** - Referral fees

### Direct Sales (High Touch)
1. **Enterprise outreach** - $200/hour consulting
2. **Trade shows** - $5k-20k per event
3. **Account executives** - $60k salary + commission
4. **Partnerships** - 30-50% rev share

## 🎯 Part 10: Decision Framework

### Should You Monetize?
✅ **YES if:**
- You have 10+ paying customers
- NPS > 40
- Churn rate < 5%/month
- Customer acquisition cost < lifetime value

### Should You Raise Funding?
✅ **YES if:**
- $50k+ MRR
- Strong team to scale
- Large market opportunity
- Need resources to compete

### Should You Build a Team?
✅ **YES if:**
- $100k+ MRR
- Can afford $150k+ in salaries
- Have clear roles & responsibilities
- Can manage people effectively

### Should You Consider Acquisition?
✅ **YES if:**
- $5M+ ARR valuation
- Strategic buyer interest
- Founders want exit
- Team retention concerns
